Send, receive and request money

O-Banking is an academic banking demo built with Django. Create an account, pass an identity check, then move money between accounts — every transfer confirmed by re-entering your password. No real funds are involved.

Open a Free Account Sign In

Transfers

Send money to another O-Banking account by account number or ID. Each transfer is confirmed by re-entering your password before it settles.

Payment requests

Ask another user for money. Requests appear in their list and can be settled or cancelled before they are paid.

Identity check

Upload an identity document and a signature. The banking screens stay locked until your details are on file.

Transaction history

Every movement is listed with its status, so it is clear what has completed and what is still pending.

About this project

O-Banking is a final-year (PFA) coursework project. It is a demonstration, not a bank: it has no connection to any payment network, it holds no real money, and accounts are limited to this installation. Balances start at zero and only move between registered users.
